# From autism knowledge to resilience: understanding the role of self-efficacy and neurodiversity attitudes of inclusive teachers in China

**作者**：Ruiqin Lin（林瑞琴）, Luodi Yu（于洛迪）, Xiaowen Wu（吴晓雯）, Jie Ren（任洁）

**发表**：*International Journal of Inclusive Education*（国际包容性教育杂志）, 2026-03-19 在线发表

**收稿**：2025-08-04 | **录用**：2026-03-10 | **在线发表**：2026-03-19

**DOI**：[10.1080/13603116.2026.2645187](https://doi.org/10.1080/13603116.2026.2645187)

## 摘要

As inclusive education expands across China, general education teachers face increasing demands in supporting students with autism spectrum sisorder (ASD) in mainstream classrooms. This study investigates the psychological mechanisms that promote teacher resilience and reduce burnout within inclusive school contexts. A moderated mediation model was tested using survey data from 272 inclusive teachers (87% female; average autism teaching experience of approximately 3 years). The analysis examined how ASD knowledge, inclusive education self-efficacy, and neurodiversity attitudes contribute to burnout outcomes. We show that greater ASD knowledge was associated with reduced teacher burnout, and this relationship was mediated by enhanced self-efficacy in inclusive teaching. Furthermore, neurodiversity-affirming attitudes significantly moderated the association between ASD knowledge and self-efficacy, such that the positive impact of knowledge was stronger among teachers with more positive attitudes toward neurodiversity. These findings highlight the value of strengths-based approaches to teacher development. They offer actionable insights for designing professional learning environments that empower educators, foster resilience, and support sustainable inclusive education practices.

随着融合教育在中国各地的推广，普通教育教师在主流课堂中支持孤独谱系障碍（ASD）学生方面面临着日益增长的需求。本研究调查了在融合学校背景下提升教师韧性并减少职业倦怠的心理机制。通过对 272 名融合教育教师（87% 为女性；平均孤独谱系教学经验约为 3 年）的调查数据，对一个有调节的中介模型进行了测试。分析探讨了 ASD 知识、融合教育自我效能感以及神经多样性态度如何影响职业倦怠结果。我们发现，更丰富的 ASD 知识与教师职业倦怠的减少相关，且这一关系是由融合教学自我效能感的增强所中介的。此外，肯定神经多样性的态度显著调节了 ASD 知识与自我效能感之间的关联，即在对神经多样性持更积极态度的教师中，知识的积极影响更为显著。这些发现强调了基于优势的教师发展方法的价值。它们为设计专业学习环境提供了可操作的见解，旨在赋能教育者、培养韧性并支持可持续的融合教育实践。
