# Eugenics - Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy

First published Wed Jul 2, 2014; substantive revision Thu Jun 2, 2022

Source: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/eugenics/

The SEP entry on Eugenics was used to enrich the wiki page [[优生学]]. The entry covers:

1. Short history of eugenics (Galton, UK, US, Nazi Germany, post-WWII)
2. Arguing for "liberal" eugenics (distinguishing old/new, reprogenetic technologies, disease vs non-disease traits, permissibility vs obligation)
3. Criticisms of liberal eugenics (comparison problem, promoting injustice, inconsistency, disability rights, undermining core values)
