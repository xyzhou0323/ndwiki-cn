## Are Computers turning NTs autistic?

In Enforcing Normalcy, Lennard Davis plays with the (self-styledly) "somewhat prel»sterous suggestion that Europe became deaf during the eighteen century" (p51). Would it be any more preposterous to suggest that if the 18th century turned the hearing into the culturally Deaf, then the cyber age is turning NT's into the culturally Autistic? ## Method

Bearing in mind that no research is free of bias, that we may well be doing little more than telling stories primarily ourselves, that we cannot to be separate from that which we obsewe, I have been aware of the dangers of being so enthusiastic about some theory that I want to promulgate that I ignore the realities of people's lives. Thus I have tried to give a balanced representation ofthe needs and wants ofpeople at both ends of the spectrum.

I joined InLv Over a year ago at the invitation of the list owner, Martijn Dekker. I have viewed myself more as a participant than an observer, with my research interest being minor to my desire to be part Of the community.
