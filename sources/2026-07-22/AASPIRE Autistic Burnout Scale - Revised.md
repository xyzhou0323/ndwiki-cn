## AASPIRE 孤独谱系耗竭量表 – 修订版：概述

## 描述

该工具用于识别和测量慢性孤独谱系耗竭。

## 开发

AASPIRE孤独谱系耗竭项目团队采用社区参与式研究（CBPR）方法，于2019年创建了AASPIRE孤独谱系耗竭量表原始版本。原始孤独谱系耗竭量表中的条目基于他们关于孤独谱系耗竭的定性研究结果（Raymaker 等，2020）。

我们目前推荐使用孤独谱系耗竭量表修订版，该版本为纳入AASPIRE成果项目而进行了修订。成果项目团队正采用社区参与式研究方法：1) 创建并验证 AASPIRE Measurement Toolkit；2) 了解哪些因素能预测结局随时间的改变（Nicolaidis 等，2026a）。

AASPIRE孤独谱系耗竭量表的修订内容包括：

● 缩短工具· 添加文字以明确时间框架· 进行小幅修改以提高可及性· 添加详细描述和情景描述以更清晰定义概念，以及 添加关于症状初始发生的条目。

AASPIRE Measurement Toolkit中的每个工具都有自我报告和照护者报告版本。AASPIRE针对伴有或不伴有智力残障的孤独谱系成年人开发并测试了这些工具，但它们也可用于一般人群或其他可能需要认知无障碍调查工具的人群。

AASPIRE成果项目的社区‑学术团队合作修订了该工具，以纳入AASPIRE Measurement Toolkit。

请参见下方关于原始版与改编版工具之间的更详细比较。

## AASPIRE Autistic Burnout Scale – Revised: Overview

## Description

This instrument identifies and measures chronic autistic burnout.

## Development

The AASPIRE Burnout Project team used a community based participatory research (CBPR) approach to create the original version of AASPIRE Autistic Burnout Scale in 2019. Items in the original Autistic Burnout Scale are based on the results of their qualitative study about autistic burnout (Raymaker et al., 2020).

We are currently recommending use of the revised version of the Autistic Burnout Scale, which was revised for inclusion in the AASPIRE Outcomes Project. The Outcomes Project team is using a CBPR approach to 1) create and validate the AASPIRE Measurement Toolkit; and 2) understand what predicts changes in outcomes over time (Nicolaidis et al., 2026a).

Revisions to the AASPIRE Burnout Scale included:

 Shortening the instrument

 Adding text to clarify the timeframe

 Making minor changes to increase accessibility

 Adding detailed descriptions and vignettes to more clearly define concepts, and

 Adding items about the onset of symptoms.

Each instrument in the AASPIRE Measurement Toolkit has self- and caregiver-reported versions. AASPIRE developed and tested the instruments with and for autistic adults with or without intellectual disability, but they could also be used with general populations or other people who may need cognitively accessible survey instruments.

The AASPIRE Outcomes Project community-academic team collaboratively revised this instrument for inclusion in the AASPIRE Measurement Toolkit.

See below for a more detailed comparison between the original and adapted instrument.

## 评分

孤独谱系耗竭量表的症状严重程度部分中的每个项目均采用5点李克特量表评分，从0（非常不同意）到4（非常同意）。总分为第1至14项之和。得分范围为0至56分，分数越高表示耗竭越严重。附加项目用于临床或研究目的，不计入总分。暂定以下截断值用于判断临床显著性孤独谱系耗竭：0‑22分为低概率；23‑32分为中等概率；33分及以上为高概率。这些解释应始终结合临床判断使用。

## 心理测量学特性

Each item on the Symptom Severity section of the Autistic Burnout Scale is scored on a 5-point Likert scale, from 0 (Strongly Disagree) to 4 (Strongly Agree). The total score is the sum of items 1-14. Scores can range from 0 to 56, with higher scores indicating more severe burnout. Additional items are intended for clinical or research purposes and are not part of the total score. Tentatively, we recommend using the following cut-offs for clinically significant burnout: 0-22 low probability; 23-32 intermediate probability; 33 and above: high probability. These interpretations should always be combined with clinical discretion.

原始版AASPIRE 孤独谱系耗竭量表表现出良好的心理测量学特性，尽管其主要通过互联网招募的样本进行测试（Bougoure等人，2025年；Mantzalas等人，2024年）。

修订版正在作为AASPIRE Outcomes Study的一部分进行心理测量学测试，这是一项纵向队列研究，追踪\~850 孤独谱系成年人的异质性样本随时间的变化（Nicolaidis等人，2026a）。

认知访谈数据证明了强大的内容效度。调查数据展示了极好的内部一致性信度（alpha=0.95）和两周重测信度（组内相关系数为0.92），强收敛效度和区分效度，以及良好的六个月变化反应度（Nicolaidis等人，2026b）。

初步分析支持该量表具有单因子和良好的结构效度（Nicolaidis等人，2026a）。

关于该量表结构和结构效度的额外结果尚待发表，将在可用时在AASPIRE Measurement Toolkit网站上分享。

AASPIRE孤独谱系耗竭量表修订版可用于非商业、临床和研究场合，使用时需注明出处。进行工具改编、修改及盈利性使用需获得许可。如需许可咨询，请联系 Christina Nicolaidis 和 AASPIRE，邮箱：info@aaspire.org

## Psychometric Properties

The original AASPIRE Autistic Burnout Scale showed strong psychometric properties, though it was tested primarily with samples recruited from the internet (Bougoure et al., 2025; Mantzalas et al., 2024).

The revised version is being psychometrically tested as part of the AASPIRE Outcomes Study, a longitudinal cohort study following a heterogeneous sample of \~850 autistic adults over time (Nicolaidis et al., 2026a).

Cognitive interview data demonstrated strong content validity. Survey data have demonstrated excellent internal consistency reliability (alpha=0.95) and 2-week test-retest reliability (intraclass coefficient of 0.92), strong convergent and discriminant validity, and good 6-month responsiveness to change (Nicolaidis et al., 2026b)

Preliminary analyses support that the scale has a single factor and good construct validity (Nicolaidis et al., 2026a).

Additional results about the scale’s structural and construct validity are pending publication and will be shared on the AASPIRE Measurement Toolkit site when available.

## Information on what it means for something to have been going on for at least 3 months

 These things could have been going on for 3 months or they could have been going on for years.

 It’s ok if you don’t remember exactly how long they have been going on.

 They don’t have to happen every single day for you to include them.

 Don’t include things that have only been going on for a few days or weeks.

## 如何将经历与他们所谓的“常态”进行比较

 “通常”指的是你认为对他们来说正常或最具代表性的情况。这可能与他们当前的实际状况相符，也可能不符。 如果你认为他们在这些方面存在很大差异，那也没关系。请思考当前的差异与通常的差异之间是否存在区别。

## How to compare experiences to what is “usual” for them

 “Usual” means whatever you consider normal for them, or to be most typical for them. This may or may not be what’s true for them currently.

 If you think they have a lot of variation in these things, that is okay. Think about whether there’s a difference between the current variation and the usual variation.
